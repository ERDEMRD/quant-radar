StochLOB: Heavy-Tailed Score Matching for Robust Limit Order Book Trend Prediction

📊 Piyasa Mikroyapısı & LOB⚙️ Execution & İşlem MaliyetiHarvard

Özet (otomatik çeviri): Score-based diffusion methods usually perturb data with Brownian Gaussian noise, corresponding to a light-tailed forward stochastic differential equation (SDE). For market time series this SDE can be a poor abstraction: limit order book (LOB) order flow is heavy-tailed, bursty, and occasionally jump-like. An exploratory Feishu A-share transfer study further tests whether the probability outputs remain usable under transaction costs and trading-rule constraints. Code and configuration files are available at https://github.com/ArshiAbolghasemi/Penny.
